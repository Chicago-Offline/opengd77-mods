The Chicago mod

OpenGD77 · MD-UV380 / RT3S

Both mods in one build. This is what's actually on my radios.

The two mods on this site started as separate itches and ended up solving the same problem from opposite ends of the screen. The split-name mod makes the channel name big enough to read at arm's length. The quieter status bar clears the header down to signal and battery. Run them together and the display stops being a dense block of small text and becomes two things you can read without stopping.

They're genuinely independent — different files, different settings, no shared state — so you can take either one on its own from its own page. This build is just the convenience of not applying two patches.

What you get

Both features, both off by default, each behind its own menu item in display options: 

Text size     1  stock
              2  two-line split name
              3  two-line split name, wide font

Status hdr    1  stock header
              2  S-meter and battery
              3  S-meter and battery, power bar on transmit

A radio you flash and don't touch behaves like a stock one. Nothing turns itself on, and either mod can go back to 1 independently of the other.

The write-ups for each live on their own pages — what the split actually keys off, and why the status glyph colours are hardcoded instead of themed. Worth reading before you flash, because both explain behaviour that isn't obvious from the menu.

Get the patch

chioff-chicago-mod.patch — both mods as one unified diff against the OpenGD77 R20260131 release source. Four files, nothing binary in it. 

git apply --3way chioff-chicago-mod.patch

Run it from the root of your OpenGD77 checkout. It touches uiUtilities.c, uiChannelMode.c, menuDisplayOptions.c and settings.h — display code only, nothing in the RF or codec paths.

If you'd rather have just one of them, the single-mod patches are on their own pages. Don't apply those and this one; this patch already contains both.

Or just flash the build

chioff-chicago-mod-R20260131.bin — OpenGD77 R20260131 with both mods and nothing else. 806,800 bytes, for MD-UV380 / RT3S. 

sha256  a65c28b176f5361947ce38445d0eb43a365f8f2325948cbfb8228b79476f1746
md5     6f48ad0c48c643e8e05823f7caac503b

Flash it with grid.radio/opengd77 — Custom File tab, pick the .bin, leave the codec donor on Built-in. There's no AMBE vocoder in this image; the OpenGD77 sources don't contain one, so that region of flash is left empty and your flashing tool merges a vocoder in from its own donor at write time.
